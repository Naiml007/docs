Naimur OpenCode Agents is a ready-to-drop-in configuration pack for the [opencode](https://opencode.ai) AI coding platform. It gives you a smart orchestrator that automatically routes every task to the right specialist — whether you're writing Rust, building a Godot game, deploying a Discord bot, or scaffolding a full-stack web app. Drop the agents and skills into your project and get an expert coding team out of the box.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the configuration files">
    Clone or copy the `agents/`, `skills/`, and `opencode.json` files into your project directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open opencode in your project">
    Launch opencode in your project root. The orchestrator loads automatically as the default agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your task">
    Tell the orchestrator what you want to build or fix. It inspects your repo and routes the work to the right specialist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and iterate">
    The code-reviewer agent checks the completed implementation for bugs and security issues before you ship.
  </Step>
</Steps>
